# call/0060: a teaching-layer change re-baselines in place, and the record keeps the trail

- Status: accepted
- Scope: agentic-host, and the host-template spine it carries
- Date: 2026-10-17
- Relates: plan/0100, plan/0090, plan/0091, call/0059, the append-only rule in the root manual

## Context and problem

The operator asked for a "good forgetting" modality: a paradigm change whose
teaching surfaces carry no hint that the change was ever a change, beginning
with the address possessive (`lemu` = you, `lemr` = your). Taken whole, the
ask collides with three load-bearing rules: MEMORY.md is append-only with one
narrow map-only exception, the certification of plan/0091 measured a named
list of forms and must not silently cover a different one, and the
`UPGRADING.md` ledger is the spine's memory, without which adopters cannot
date their own manuals. Taken as a statement about the teaching layer, the ask
is already house practice: the zh partials left the manuals wholesale with the
doctrine kept whole, and the `lself` ruling corrected the manual in place
while the adjudication went to memory.

## Decision

The modality is adopted in bounded form, named honestly as re-baselining with
an audit trail.

- The teaching layer (manuals, skills, vocabulary, classifier tables, the
  manual's exemplar exchanges) states the paradigm in present tense. No
  "newly added" markers, no transition notes, no deprecation text.
- The enforcement instruments carry no transition state: no warning tier for
  a retired form, no sunset schedule, no scheduled escalation. A paradigm
  rule enforces at full strength from the moment its entry lands.
- The record layer stays append-only exactly as the standing rules require:
  memory entries, decisions, closed plans, and receipts are never struck.
- A measurement names the form list it measured; a paradigm change either
  re-measures or scopes the standing chart by name, never silently.
- The ledger entry carries the version truth: a cold reader dates any manual
  by reconciling it against the ledger, which is why the teaching layer may
  forget safely.

First test case: the address possessive of plan/0100.

## Consequences

- A future paradigm change on this host follows the same shape: rule it
  (cast, then a blind bench where the question admits one), re-baseline the
  teaching layer, append the record, scope the measurement, ledger the entry.
- An unrecorded forgetting is indistinguishable from an error; the decision
  authorizing forgetting is itself permanent record and is never struck by
  the modality it authorizes.
- The bench's named cost of the no-transition axiom (old and new prose can
  coexist without a marker) is answered by the ledger, not by the instruments.
