## The reading

**No measurable difference between the guidance conditions** at this N with this writer: two warning events across eighteen first drafts, one in the control and one in the exemplar condition, none in the negative-list condition: a difference of one event, which is noise. The negative-list condition did not prime the banned words (none of the three conditions produced any word-choice trope at all). Both events were **structural** tropes: a participial tail in the control and a false range in the exemplar condition: style shapes, not vocabulary.

That last fact is the experiment's one solid finding, and it inverts the question back: **for a strong writer, the word-choice list is not the battleground: the structural tropes are.** The vocabulary the issue family argues about never fired in any arm.

**The prohibition list is the best guidance for the weak model: by four times.** It cut every trope family, including the structural families it never named (the ing-tails dropped two-thirds; the false-ranges went to zero): naming the vocabulary ban appears to prime general care, not merely word avoidance. No banned word leaked into any condition: the vocabulary war never fired in any arm; the war is structural, and the list fights structure better than the exemplars do.
