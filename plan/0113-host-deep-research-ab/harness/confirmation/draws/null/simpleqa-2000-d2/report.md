# Who accompanied Lilian Ngoyi on the illegal journey to Lausanne?

## Answer

**Dora Tamana** accompanied Lilian Ngoyi on the 1955 illegal journey to Lausanne,
Switzerland, to attend the World Congress of Mothers convened by the Women's
International Democratic Federation (WIDF).

## Evidence

The two women travelled as the official delegates of the Federation of South African
Women (FEDSAW), and because neither could obtain a passport they attempted to leave the
country secretly — stowing away on a ship out of Cape Town under false ("white") names.

- Lilian Ngoyi "embarked on an illegal journey to Lausanne, Switzerland, in order to
  participate in the World Congress of Mothers ... held by the Women's International
  Democratic Federation (WIDF). Accompanied by her fellow activist Dora Tamana, and as an
  official delegate of FEDSAW, she embarked on a journey that would see an attempt to
  stow away on a boat leaving Cape Town under 'white names'".
  — [Wikipedia: Lillian Ngoyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lillian_Ngoyi)
- "The Federation of South African Women chose two delegates to attend the Congress of
  Mothers organized by the Women's International Democratic Federation (WIDF) in 1955.
  Dora Tamana accompanies fellow delegate and founder of FEDSAW, Lilian Ngoyi to the
  Congress ... Neither she nor Ngoyi had official passports."
  — [Wikipedia: Dora Tamana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dora_Tamana)
- "Together with Dora Tamana, she was arrested while trying to board a ship to a
  conference in Switzerland without a passport."
  — [South African History Online: Lillian Masediba Ngoyi](https://www.sahistory.org.za/people/lilian-masediba-ngoyi)

## Notes

The two delegates also visited other countries (England, Germany, Switzerland, Romania,
China and the Soviet Union) on the same trip before returning to South Africa. Ngoyi and
Tamana were lifelong collaborators in FEDSAW and the anti-pass movement; Ngoyi went on to
co-lead the 9 August 1956 women's march to the Union Buildings.
