# Paris: the Seine with its bridges and footbridges

La Rédaction Paris je t'aime \-

37 bridges and footbridges span the Seine, connecting the Left Bank to the Right Bank.

With the exception of a few newer bridges, most of the bridges over the Seine are reminders of Paris past.

The Parisii tribe who inhabited **Ile de la Cité** built the first bridges in Paris, the Petit Pont and the Grand Pont, in 52 BC. Today **there are 37 bridges spanning the Seine, which flows for 13 km through Paris**. They make for an exceptional heritage and are a magnificent sight, by day as by night.

The **Seine riverbanks** – from [Pont de Sully](https://parisjetaime.com/eng/transport/pont-de-sully-p1870) to [Pont de Bir-Hakeim](https://parisjetaime.com/eng/transport/pont-de-bir-hakeim-p1880) via [Pont Alexandre III](https://parisjetaime.com/eng/transport/pont-alexandre-iii-p1875) – are a **UNESCO world heritage site**. The best known bridges in Paris include the oldest one, [Pont Neuf](https://parisjetaime.com/eng/transport/pont-neuf-p1877), the construction of which began in 1578; **Pont Alexandre III**, built for the 1900 Universal Exhibition, [Pont Marie](https://parisjetaime.com/eng/transport/pont-marie-p1882), built to provide access to Ile Saint Louis when it began to be urbanized in the 17th century, and [Pont de l'Alma](https://parisjetaime.com/eng/transport/pont-de-l-alma-p1878), a magnet for tourists, as it is the place where Princess Diana met her tragic death in 1997\.

The[ Pont des Arts](https://parisjetaime.com/eng/transport/pont-des-arts-passerelle-des-arts-p3682), which connects the **Institut de France** to the **Louvre Palace**, has become a meeting place for lovers from all over the world. The most recent footbridge, the [Passerelle Simone de Beauvoir](https://parisjetaime.com/eng/transport/passerelle-simone-de-beauvoir-p1867), is a lens-shaped structure that was inaugurated in 2006\.
