    River in France
    At the mouth it carries 100 m³/s on average: an Olympic pool every 25 seconds. It drops from 321 m to 31 m, 0.6 m per kilometre on average. Its basin covers 12,800 km², about the size of BS. 2.2 million people live in it. Highest water: from December to February. Dams and reservoirs hold back a large share of its water (13% of a year's flow).
