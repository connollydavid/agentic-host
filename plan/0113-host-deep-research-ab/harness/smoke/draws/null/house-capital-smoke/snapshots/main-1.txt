**Paris**[\[b\]](#cite_note-8) is the [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_city "Capital city") and [largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_communes_in_France_with_over_20,000_inhabitants "List of communes in France with over 20,000 inhabitants") of [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France "France"), with an estimated city population of 2\.04 million in an area of 105\.4 km2 (40.7 sq mi), as of January 2026[\[update\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Paris&action=edit),[\[4\]](#cite_note-Pop.Paris-5) and a metropolitan population of 13\.3 million (2023). Located on the [Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine "Seine") in the centre of the [Île-de-France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Île-de-France "Île-de-France") region, it is the [largest metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_metropolitan_areas_in_Europe "List of metropolitan areas in Europe") and [fourth-most populous city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_the_European_Union_by_population_within_city_limits "List of cities in the European Union by population within city limits") in the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union "European Union") (EU). Paris is nicknamed the "City of Light" mainly due to its leading role in the [Age of Enlightenment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Enlightenment "Age of Enlightenment").

Administratively, Paris is divided into [twenty arrondissements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrondissements_of_Paris "Arrondissements of Paris") (districts), each with their own cultural identity. [Haussmann's renovation of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haussmann's_renovation_of_Paris "Haussmann's renovation of Paris"), which created new boulevards, parks, and public works, gave birth to a modern city known as the "[capital of the 19th century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris,_Capital_of_the_19th_Century "Paris, Capital of the 19th Century")". Paris is a major railway, motorway, and air-transport hub; in 2024 [Charles de Gaulle Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_de_Gaulle_Airport "Charles de Gaulle Airport") was the EU's [busiest airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_the_busiest_airports_in_Europe "List of the busiest airports in Europe"). Paris has one of the most [sustainable transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_transportation "Sustainable transportation") systems in the world and is one of only two cities that have received the [Sustainable Transport Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_Transport_Award "Sustainable Transport Award") twice. Its [Art Nouveau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_Nouveau "Art Nouveau")\-decorated [Métro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Métro "Paris Métro") has become a symbol of the city. Paris is known for its museums and architectural landmarks: the [Louvre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louvre "Louvre") is the largest and most-visited museum in the world; the [Musée d'Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musée_d'Orsay "Musée d'Orsay"), [Musée Marmottan Monet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musée_Marmottan_Monet "Musée Marmottan Monet"), and [Musée de l'Orangerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musée_de_l'Orangerie "Musée de l'Orangerie") are noted for their collections of French [Impressionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impressionism "Impressionism") art, while the [Musée National d'Art Moderne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musée_National_d'Art_Moderne "Musée National d'Art Moderne"), [Musée Rodin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musée_Rodin "Musée Rodin"), and [Musée Picasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musée_Picasso "Musée Picasso") are noted for their collections of [modern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_art "Modern art") and [contemporary art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemporary_art "Contemporary art"). Parts of the city along the Seine have been designated a [UNESCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO "UNESCO") [World Heritage Site](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Heritage_Site "World Heritage Site") since 1991[\[update\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Paris&action=edit).

The [president of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_France "President of France") and both houses of the [French Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Parliament "French Parliament") sit in Paris.
