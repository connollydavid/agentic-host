# The capital of France and the river that runs through it

**Question:** What is the capital of France, and which river runs through it?

## Answer

The capital of France is **Paris**, and the river that runs through it is the **Seine** (Seine River, French: *la Seine*).

## Findings

### Paris is the capital of France

Paris is the capital and largest city of France, with an estimated city population of 2.04 million in an area of 105.4 km2 (40.7 sq mi), as of January 2026, and a metropolitan population of 13.3 million (2023). ([Wikipedia – Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris))

The UNESCO World Heritage listing for "Paris, Banks of the Seine" likewise describes the historical superposition of layers of "Paris, the capital city of one of the first great nation states of Europe". ([UNESCO World Heritage Centre](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/600/))

### The Seine River runs through Paris

The Seine is a 777-kilometre-long (483 mi) river in northern France. ([Wikipedia – Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine))

It rises at Source-Seine, 30 kilometres (19 mi) northwest of Dijon in northeastern France in the Langres plateau, flowing through Paris and into the English Channel at Le Havre (and Honfleur on the left bank). ([Wikipedia – Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine))

Paris itself is described by Wikipedia as "Located on the Seine in the centre of the Île-de-France region", and in its Geography section the article states that "Paris is located in northern central France, in a north-bending arc of the river Seine, whose crest includes two islands, the Île Saint-Louis and the larger Île de la Cité, which form the oldest part of Paris." ([Wikipedia – Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris))

UNESCO corroborates this: "The city of Paris is built along a bend in the River Seine, between the confluence of the Marne and the Oise Rivers." ([UNESCO World Heritage Centre](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/600/))
