 IEEE Frank Rosenblatt Award
Nominations for the next IEEE Frank Rosenblatt Award are due 15 January as part of the IEEE Technical Field Awards nomination cycle.
Learn more about the IEEE Frank Rosenblatt Award | Submit a nomination
The IEEE Frank Rosenblatt Award is a Technical Field Award sponsored by the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society.
The award, established in 2004, is named in honor of Frank Rosenblatt, who is regarded as one of the founders of neural networks. Basing his research on study of fly vision, he developed the single-layer input layer and an output layer of neural cells. Frequent presentation of a pattern or patterns resulted in changes in the input to output connections, facilitating future recognition of these patterns, or memory. His work influenced and anticipated many modern neural network approaches.
This award will be presented for outstanding contributions o the advancement of the design, practice, techniques or theory in biologically and linguistically motivated computational paradigms including but not limited to neural networks, connectionist systems, evolutionary computation, fuzzy systems, and hybrid intelligent systems in which these paradigms are contained.
This award may be presented to an individual, multiple recipients, or a team of not more than three members. This award is administered by the Technical Field Awards Council of the IEEE Awards Board. Prize items include a bronze medal, certificate and honorarium.

 IEEE CIS Neural Networks Pioneer Award Nomination Instructions 
Individuals interested in submitting a nomination for the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society Neural Networks Pioneer Award are invited to prepare and submit a Nomination Letter (a single PDF file) via the IEEE CIS Awards Nomination online form by April 30. The name of the file must be "surname_of_nominee-NNPioneer.pdf". Self-nominations are not allowed. 
The Nomination Letter includes all of the following required materials:
1. Nominator Information
Provide:
Name
Affiliation
Email address
2. Nominee Information
Provide:
Name
Affiliation
Postal address
Email address
Webpage/URL
3. Category of Nomination
Identify the category of pioneering contribution:
Fundamental understanding, or
Engineering application
Note: The total nomination package should describe how this individual contributed to the advancement of theory, technologies, and/or applications of neural networks by inventing new technologies, creating innovative technical developments, implementing new products, or managing innovative product design or production processes.
4. Proposed Citation
Submit a complete, correct, and concise proposed citation for the nominee.
Note: The Awards Committee reserves the right to revise the citation as needed.
5. Reference Letters
Include up to five (5) reference letters supporting the nomination.
Important:
Incomplete submissions or missing required materials may negatively affect the evaluation of the nomination.
Selection/Basis for Judging
Significance of contribution in the field of neural networks made at least 15 years prior to award date. The Pioneer Award is not a lifetime achievement award and will be judged by the following criteria:
The technical contribution(s) must have occurred at least 15 years prior to the year of the Award. 
There shall be between one three major technical contributions, which have realized impact and verifiable evidence. 
The impact and evidence of those contributions, as well as possible spinoffs (e.g. patents or follow-up papers), might be more recent, but they must be clearly associated with the original contribution. 
The pioneering nature of cited contribution in light of the category of nomination, the impact on the industry, economy, government, and society, and the impact on neural networks research and development from the point of view of theory, technologies, and/or applications.
Proposed Citation: The committee will evaluate the citation submitted with the nomination for completeness, accuracy, and succinctness. The Awards Committee reserves the right to make any necessary change to the citation.
Up to 5 reference letters supporting the nomination will be reviewed.
