Why

Research reaches the record as findings, not as sources. The house's plans cite what a run concluded, and nothing between a fetched page and a plan line verifies that the page said it. The five candidates answer that in five different ways, from a ledger plus a checker to a confidence ladder that the same model awards lself, and the methodology can test them: the same tasks, the same pinned harness, the same model, and a mechanical rubric.

The endpoint that carried the installed research skills retired in November, so the house has no working deep-research tool. The incumbent is therefore the null arm, and a candidate that cannot beat a plain agent with the built-in web tools has not earned the vendoring, the dependency tree and the token cost.

The re-run rule

A draw that fails because the harness, an adapter, the search provider or the driver broke is a harness break, not an arm result. It is void, the breakage is recorded, the harness is fixed, and the draw is re-run once under the same task and config; the first attempt stays in the record with a rerun.json naming the break. A budget-exhausted draw is an arm result: void, recorded, never re-run, because re-running the same cap loops. An arm whose native output shape cannot satisfy the contract is an exclusion with evidence. A low score is never a re-run reason; this rule is fixed before the redo.

An adapter that excludes a draw whose native output already carries a fetched source record is a harness break: hffmnnj finding notes, kanatami worker results with a fetched page, or a samber/weizhena report whose cited pages are in the run's fetch log with a quote and locator. The report's own source list is not a fetched page. The smoke fails it, the adapter is fixed, and the draw is re-run.

The rubric

Mechanical where it can be; the hard gate is a count.

  1. Phantom sources, the hard gate. Every cited URL must appear in the run's own fetch log. A cited source the run never fetched is invented. Zero tolerance: a draw with one is void.
  2. Cited-quote support, six points. Each cited sentence must carry a quote and a locator, and the quote must appear in the run's snapshot of that page after whitespace and unicode normalisation. The pass threshold is frozen before the draws.
  3. Known-answer correctness, four points. Normalised match against the frozen key.
  4. Must-cover recall, four points. Share of the pre-registered entities the report names.
  5. Numerals, three points. A numeral must appear in a cited snapshot. Each unsupported numeral counts against the draw.
  6. Prose, three points. The house's prose lane over the report, inverted into a score.

Cost is recorded, not scored: completion and prompt tokens, tool calls, wall time, and the exit status and contract compliance of each draw.

The judge is Opus 5.5 through the host's claude CLI (claude-agent/opus, resolved claude-opus-5-5). It scores completeness and creativity, 0-4 each, on the blind report and ledger; the judge score is 0-8. The pooled selection score is the mechanical score (0-16) plus the judge score (0-24). The judge's model and route are checked against the pin, its validity gate must pass, and it retries on a transient failure; a failing judge contributes 0. With no passing judge, the mechanical score stands alone. The confirmation runs the same judge on the held-out draws and records both sub-scores.

The protocol

  • Selection is one frozen round, because the budget bounds it: every arm draws once on every selection task (5 × 6 = 30 draws), interleaved by task. A re-baseline would need a new pre-declared round before its draws; numbers do not compare across rounds. The first attempt's round is recorded as harness-defective; the corrected round is the #selection-redo task.
  • Lock. The pooled selection score is mechanical (0-16) plus judge (0-8), a 24-point maximum. Among arms within 1.5 points of the best, only a candidate with at least one valid draw and a pre-declared null-relative clearance may lock; the tie-break is the six-row suite-fit evidence table, then the judge score, then name order. The judge's model, route and validity gate are checked. The token count is recorded, never a tie-break.
  • Confirmation pits the locked arm against null, on the fresh held-out tasks, with two fresh draws per held-out task per arm (2 × 2 × 5 = 20 draws) and randomised order within each task block. The locked arm is read from round-redo.json, never weizhena; the fresh set is sealed with its own key; --key is required and --check does not decrypt. A void is a gate, not a measured zero. The pre-declared criteria: a margin of 1.0 point, a per-task floor of 8 of 16, zero phantom sources, no foreign-model reply, every draw valid or a recorded harness-break re-run, and the judge's completeness and creativity recorded.
  • Ship. The component ships the confirmed arm when every criterion passes, and prints SHIP; otherwise it prints NO-SHIP, reports the criteria it missed, and the incumbent (null) stands. The operator may direct another attempt; the criteria are not moved to fit a result.
  • Refinement after a ship is a weco optimisation pass on the confirmed arm, or on the shim, scored with the selection set. A refined variant has to re-confirm on the held-out set before it ships, for the same winner's-curse reason the confirmation stage exists.

The suite-fit score

Scored mechanically against this design when the arm is locked, out of six. These are the integrations that make the winner a component of this suite rather than a skill dropped into it.

  1. Every role runs on the reference model through models, model, or the session model, without a config edit.
  2. It produces an evidence ledger the rubric can read: claims, quotes and locators, machine-readable.
  3. Source snapshots can be pinned and digested through the run contract.
  4. Artifacts land under the run directory, never in the home directory.
  5. One command runs a task: at most one human gate inside a draw.
  6. The vendored tree is reproducible and its license file permits it: a lockfile for the runtime, and a license at the pinned commit.

A shippable arm scores at least four, and clears the first and the sixth.

The selection record carries a six-row table, one row per criterion, with the evidence for every arm in the band. A suite-fit score without the row is not a score.

The component's licensing

  • Our own files are Unlicense: the shim, the run contract, the checker, the kit and the tasks the house writes. The repo carries the Unlicense text and the manifest field.
  • The shimmed arm keeps its own: pinned and unmodified, its LICENSE and its copyright notices intact, listed in the third-party notice with pin, SPDX id and the path to its notice.
  • House adaptations live in house files, never as edits to upstream files.
  • A copyleft arm runs out of process, behind an arm's-length boundary such as host-reference-openscad's (call/0033).
