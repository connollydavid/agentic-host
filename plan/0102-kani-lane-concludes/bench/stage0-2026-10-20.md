## The code (read at the pin, 90a0d71)

The three heavy harnesses drive `scan_lem_line` whole: a tokenizer loop with
quote state, per-word classification (exact compares, `is_first_person_word`,
`starts_with("lem")`, `LEM_WHITELIST.contains`, `LEM_CANONICAL.contains`), and
an agreement lookahead that scans ahead for a verb and matches it against a
four-arm table. The input bounds are already tiny (n <= 8, n <= 8, n <= 12
symbolic bytes over restricted alphabets), so the bound is not the problem.

The verification condition is the whole composition: every tokenizer path times
every classification branch times a thirteen-entry table of string equalities
over symbolically-derived words times the lookahead, and the harness asserts
re-run the same table lookups on the hit words, which doubles the surface. That is
why six hours buys nothing.

Two coverage defects ride along: at n <= 8 the proof can never reach
`lemuselves` (ten bytes), the longest canonical entry, so the longest forms sit
outside the stated domain; and none of the three carries an unwind annotation
while the small rungs do.

## The fix class

Decomposition with function contracts (the pinned kani, `-Z function-contracts`):

- Extract `word_run_end(b, i)` (the maximal word-run scan) and
  `lem_word_kinds(word, quoted, rest)` (the entire per-word decision including
  the agreement lookahead, pure over the word's bytes, the quote flag, and the
  bytes after the word). `scan_lem_line` becomes framing plus calls;
  behavior-identical, guarded by the golden tests, the proptest suite, and the
  conformance lane.
- Contracts: `word_run_end` ensures the returned index is in bounds and either
  ends the slice or lands on a non-word byte; `lem_word_kinds` ensures no panic
  and the soundness invariants (a Form kind is canonical and not bare `l`; a
  Mangle kind starts with lem and is neither canonical nor whitelisted; a
  FirstPerson kind is a first-person word).
- Word-level harnesses prove the contracts (`kani::proof_for_contract`), one
  word at n <= 10: full table coverage including `lemuselves`, a fraction of
  the old state space.
- Line-level harnesses (`scan_line_never_panics`, `hits_are_word_bounded`,
  framed over n <= 8 and n <= 12 as today) verify against the contract-stubbed
  bodies: the tables leave their verification conditions.
- Every harness carries `#[kani::unwind(K)]`; the CI job grows one matrix row
  per harness and runs with `-Z function-contracts`.

If contracts misbehave in this crate, the fallback is the same decomposition
without body stubbing: word-level proofs stand alone, the line-level framing
rides tests and conformance, and that restatement comes back to the operator as
a ruling before it lands.

### The playbook if the classifier contract caps (pre-written)

The expensive fact is table membership; assumptions prune it cheaply where
proofs must establish it. Split the one contract proof into three by word shape, each proving only
its own arm. Over canonical entries: the form flag is exact. Over
uncanonical lem-prefixed words: the mangle flag is exact against the
whitelist. The first-person arm is unchanged. Each table scan then appears only in an assume, the direction
SAT solvers like. The line-level stubs are unchanged: the contract on the
function is the same, only its proof is sharded.

Measured 2026-10-20, local kani 0.67.0 (the CI pin), sequential runs under
thirty-minute caps (log preserved from the run):

- `scan_line_never_panics`: started 04:04:05, cap fired 04:34:05, **no
  verdict**: thirty minutes buys nothing locally either.
- `form_and_mangle_hits_are_classified_by_the_table`: started 04:34:05, killed
  at the one-minute mark to free the source for the fix. The `FAILED` line the
  kill left in the log is an artifact, not a counterexample: the harness

  semantically impossible; a killed solver exits nonzero and the truncated
  output parsed as a verdict. GitHub's own timing is this harness's
  measurement: six hours, cancelled.
- `hits_are_word_bounded`: never started; GitHub's six-hour cancellation is
  its measurement.

The design is identical under every one of these numbers: the composition, not
any single harness, is what six hours cannot buy.
